About Manka Farms

Your Enchanting
Jungle &
Farmhouse Escape

Manka Farm is nestled in the wilderness of Pench Tiger Reserve, hardly 7 km away from Turia Gate — a home away from home where you can enjoy tranquility amidst sprawling wildscapes and greens. Nothing can quite describe the unusual opulence and picturesque setting of this beautiful home.

Trek along, enveloped in hues of green on both sides on an offbeat track. Far away from the hustle of city life, this is where local culinary delights are created and served, much to the delight of your taste buds — a land unexplored, kept away from the rush of unruly crowds, where silence vies with charm.

Good to Know

Property Guide
& House Rules

On Arrival & Stay

  • 15 KVA generator backs up all lights and fans during a power outage; a 1 KVA inverter covers 1 light and 1 fan per room for up to 3 hours.
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi and fair mobile network coverage throughout the property, subject to availability.
  • Non-vegetarian food is welcome — please share meal preferences at least 48 hours before check-in. Guests do not have direct access to the villa kitchen.
  • CCTV covers common and external areas; an in-room safe and security alarms are provided for peace of mind.
  • Entry & exit hours are 8:00 PM to 5:30 AM — please keep noise to a minimum after 10 PM.

Please Note

  • Guests below 18 years of age are not permitted without a parent. Passport, Aadhar, Driving License or a Govt. ID is required at check-in.
  • Pets are not allowed and none live on the property. Smoking within the premises is not permitted.
  • The property is not wheelchair accessible — please plan arrangements accordingly.
  • Our caretaker greets you and assists with check-in; keys are collected and deposited through on-site staff. Outside visitors are not permitted in guest rooms.
  • A barbeque grill can be arranged at an additional cost. Rates quoted are on a per-person, per-day basis.
